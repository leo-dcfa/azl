Why local

Your data never leaves the building

Local AI means the models run on machines you own, on your own network. Nothing your team types is sent to an AI company.

  • Your data stays yours

    Prompts, documents and customer records stay on hardware you own. No third-party retention policy to vet for every new tool.

  • It can’t change under you

    Hosted models are updated, repriced and retired on someone else’s schedule. Weights you own change only when you decide.

  • Costs you can plan

    You pay once for the hardware, not per token. A busy month doesn’t turn into a surprise invoice.

  • You can check it

    The exact models and settings you run are the ones we test, and they can be tested again after every change.

Should we choose DGX Sparks or a Mac Studio?

Both can grow: you add Sparks to a cluster, and Mac Studios can be wired together too. The difference is the software. DGX Sparks run NVIDIA’s CUDA stack, which most AI software is built and tested on first, and it is mature at serving many people and agents at the same time. A Mac Studio runs models on Apple’s Metal, which is newer for this work: it is quick and quiet for one or two people at a time, and slows sooner when many requests arrive together. We recommend one after seeing your tasks in the Evaluation phase.

Can you set up laptops instead of an office machine?

Yes. We set up MacBook Pros and RTX Spark laptops with local models and agents, one per person. A laptop can run its own models on the road and use the machine in your office for heavier work when it’s on your network.

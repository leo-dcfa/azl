AI sovereignty

Own your intelligence

If your business runs on AI, the models, prompts, evals and data behind it are part of what makes you valuable. When all of that lives inside someone else's platform, you're renting it. I set things up so the important parts belong to you.

  • Your IP stays yours

    Code, prompts, datasets, evals and any fine-tuned models are handed over to you, not left in my accounts or locked in a vendor's.

  • No lock-in

    Open models and standard tools mean you can change providers, or bring everything in-house, without starting again.

  • You decide where it runs

    On your own servers, in your cloud account, or on Claude where it's the right tool for the job.

Private work

Keep sensitive work off the cloud

Some jobs shouldn't go anywhere near a cloud AI provider. If you need documents analysed, a model run over sensitive data, or a fine-tuned model tested, I can run it on infrastructure I run myself and hand back the results.

Ask about private work

  • Not sent to AI companies

    Your data isn't sent to OpenAI, Anthropic or any other AI provider.

  • No servers to set up

    Useful for analysis or a one-off job where buying hardware makes no sense.

  • Agreed up front

    We agree what runs, and what happens to your data afterwards, before anything starts.

  • Long term? Your servers

    If something needs to keep running, I'll usually set it up on your own hardware instead.

How I work

Roughly how it goes

  1. We talk it through

    You tell me what you're trying to fix. If AI isn't the right tool for it, I'll say so.

  2. I build it

    You get something to try early on, then I build it out properly. Claude, a local model or both, depending on cost and how sensitive the data is.

  3. I hand it over

    With evals you can run yourself and code your team can read and maintain.
